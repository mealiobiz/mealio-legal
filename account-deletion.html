Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Mealio account and associated cloud data in the app or request deletion by email.

PrivacyTermsAccount deletion

Delete in the app

Open Mealio, go to Profile → Account & privacy → Delete account, read the warning and confirm permanent deletion. You may be asked to sign in again to protect your account.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the address connected to your Mealio account. Use the subject “Mealio account deletion” and state that you want the account and associated data deleted. We may ask you to verify control of the account before acting.

Request account deletion

Data removed

Deletion removes your Mealio authentication account, profile, nutrition profile and targets, meal plans and portions, weight entries, shopping preferences, household membership and stored profile photo. Shared household records owned by another member may remain for that owner, but your membership and personal portions are removed.

Limited retention

We may retain information required for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting obligations. Provider backups may take additional time to expire and are not used to restore a deleted account. Google Play or Apple may retain transaction records under their own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Mealio account does not cancel an app-store subscription. Cancel the subscription in Google Play or Apple before deleting your account to stop future renewal.

Timing and help

We aim to complete verified email requests within 30 days. Contact mealio.biz@gmail.com if you need help.
