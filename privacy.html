Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mealio App handles information when you use Mealio.

Effective 9 September 2026

PrivacyTermsAccount deletion

Information we collect

We store account details such as your email address, display name, optional profile photo and bio; nutrition profile details such as birth date, height, weight, sex used for nutrition equations, activity level and goals; meal plans, ingredient portions, shopping preferences, weight entries, household participation and subscription status.

How we use information

We use this information to create and sync your account, estimate nutrition targets, calculate portions and shopping requirements, show progress, support household meal planning, verify subscription access, provide support, prevent abuse and maintain the service.

Household sharing

If you join a household, household members can see household meal plans, participants and shared portions. Your body measurements, weight history and nutrition targets are not shared with household members.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage and server functions. Google Play and, when available, Apple process purchases and payments. RevenueCat receives your Mealio account identifier and store purchase information to verify subscription access. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Mealio does not receive your payment-card details.

Advertising and sale of data

Mealio does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

Storage and security

Account data is stored using Supabase infrastructure. We use access controls and encrypted network connections, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active or as needed to operate Mealio. You can delete your account in the app or request deletion using our account-deletion page. Active account data is then removed, except information we must retain for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting obligations. Provider backups may take additional time to expire. App-store transaction records are controlled by the relevant store.

Account deletion options

Your choices

You can review and update most information inside Mealio. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing us. You may leave a household without deleting your personal account.

Children

Mealio is intended for people aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly offer accounts to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Nutrition information

Mealio provides planning estimates and is not a medical service. Ingredient data, calorie targets and progress estimates may be incomplete or inaccurate. Seek qualified advice for medical or dietary needs.

Food data

Mealio contains data adapted from the Australian Food Composition Database – Release 3 by Food Standards Australia New Zealand under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Australia licence. Mealio simplifies some food names and presentation. See the AFCD Data User Licence Agreement.

Contact and complaints

Email mealio.biz@gmail.com with privacy questions, requests or complaints. We will review and respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy as Mealio changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice in the app when a material change requires it.
